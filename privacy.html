CTFM Reader Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

Overview

CTFM Reader is a magazine-reading application that provides access to Connecticut Freemason magazine issues.

Information We Collect

CTFM Reader does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the app.

CTFM Reader does not directly collect names, email addresses, telephone numbers, contacts, photographs, or other personal information from users.

Magazine Content

The app retrieves magazine information and magazine documents from internet-based services in order to display the available magazine issues to the user. These requests are used to provide the functionality of the application.

Data Sharing

CTFM Reader does not sell or share users' personal information.

Children's Privacy

CTFM Reader does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time if the application's functionality or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page.
